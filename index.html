Automated sleep staging from EEG

CentraleSupélec·Dreem Data Challenge, 2023

Classifying 30-second epochs into the five sleep stages from a five-channel EEG headband sampled at 250 Hz and a three-axis accelerometer. I built 3,390 features — spectral entropy, Hjorth parameters, Higuchi, Katz and Petrosian fractal dimensions — computed on sub-segments and on past and future rolling windows, then cut collinearity by hierarchical clustering down to 2,532 features and rebalanced the minority stages with SMOTE.

Macro F1 0.78 · accuracy 0.91 (XGBoost)

Per-class F1 runs from 0.96 on N3 to 0.36 on N1 — the transition stage on which human scorers also disagree. Joint work with Nicolas Criscuolo.

github.com/coquetl/sleep-staging-eeg →

Mechanistic modelling of a predator–prey system

CentraleSupélec·Individual research project

Reproducing and criticising a published analysis of Luckinbill's Didinium–Paramecium experiments. I fitted a Rosenzweig–MacArthur ODE model to the digitised time series, ran a Sobol sensitivity analysis to rank parameter influence, diagnosed an identifiability problem behind a singular gradient, and replaced the Holling type II functional response with a sigmoid one on biological grounds.

R² 0.55 → 0.82 · AIC 7674 → 7653

Prostate segmentation in 3D MRI

CentraleSupélec·Course project

A V-Net implemented on the Medical Segmentation Decathlon prostate dataset: a 3D convolutional encoder–decoder trained with a Dice loss, which keeps a small organ from being drowned out by background voxels the way a plain cross-entropy would.
